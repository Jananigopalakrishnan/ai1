Introduction

Powering Cellular Work

Cellular respiration is a critical collection of metabolic reactions that convert biochemical energy from nutrients into adenosine triphosphate (ATP), releasing waste products in the process.

OpenStax Biology 2e Core: "Organisms must extract energy from food molecules, storing it in high-energy phosphate bonds of ATP. The chemical equation is:"

$$C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{Energy (ATP)}$$
